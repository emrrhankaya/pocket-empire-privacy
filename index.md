# Privacy Policy for Pocket Empire

**Last updated: September 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Empire ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Pocket Empire mobile game.

By using Pocket Empire, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Pocket Empire may collect certain information automatically when you use the Game. This may include:

- Device and operating system information
- App version and technical information
- Gameplay events and interactions
- Game progress and performance information
- Advertising identifiers where permitted
- Crash reports and diagnostic information
- Advertising interactions and related information
- Purchase-related information provided by the relevant app store

We do not intentionally request information such as your name, home address, passwords, or financial account credentials through the Game.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

- Operate and improve Pocket Empire
- Save and maintain game functionality
- Understand gameplay and feature usage
- Diagnose crashes, bugs, and technical problems
- Measure game performance
- Provide and measure advertisements
- Prevent abuse and maintain the security of the Game
- Improve balancing, features, and the overall player experience
- Process and verify in-app purchases through supported app stores

## 3. Advertising

Pocket Empire uses Google AdMob to provide advertising.

The Game may offer rewarded advertisements that players can voluntarily watch in exchange for in-game benefits. The Game may also display interstitial advertisements at appropriate points during gameplay.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, and other device information in accordance with applicable law and the user's consent choices.

Where required, Pocket Empire uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent for advertising and related data processing.

For more information about how Google uses information, please review Google's Privacy & Terms documentation.

## 4. Firebase Services

Pocket Empire uses certain Google Firebase services, including:

### Firebase Analytics
Used to understand how players interact with the Game and to measure gameplay and feature performance.

### Firebase Crashlytics
Used to collect crash reports and diagnostic information so that technical problems can be identified and fixed.

### Firebase Remote Config
Used to remotely configure certain game settings and features without requiring a new version of the Game.

Information processed through these services is handled according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

## 5. Local Game Data

Some game information, such as progress, settings, currencies, upgrades, and other gameplay state, may be stored locally on your device.

Removing the Game or clearing its application data may result in the loss of locally stored progress unless a cloud-based save feature is provided in the future.

## 6. In-App Purchases

Pocket Empire may offer optional in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed by the platform through which the Game is distributed, such as Google Play or Apple's App Store. We do not directly receive or store your complete payment card or banking information.

The relevant platform provider may process purchase and payment information according to its own privacy policy.

## 7. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Pocket Empire provides users with options regarding consent for advertising and related data processing.

Users may be able to review or modify their privacy choices through privacy options made available within the Game.

Device-level advertising and privacy settings may also be available through your operating system.

## 8. Third-Party Services

Pocket Empire may use third-party services including:

- Google AdMob
- Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
- Google Firebase Analytics
- Google Firebase Crashlytics
- Google Firebase Remote Config
- Google Play services
- Apple App Store services

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

## 9. Data Retention

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the policies and retention settings of those providers.

We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to legal, security, and operational requirements.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Pocket Empire is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected in a manner that violates applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to address the issue.

Parents or guardians who have concerns may contact us using the contact information below.

## 11. International Data Processing

Because some service providers used by Pocket Empire operate internationally, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live.

Where applicable, such processing is subject to the safeguards and requirements provided by applicable data protection laws and the relevant service providers.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Pocket Empire, its services, or applicable legal requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
